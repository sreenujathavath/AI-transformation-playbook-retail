## The case

**i-Q Retail** is a mid-size specialty retailer (apparel, home goods, lifestyle accessories) with about 8,000 employees, $2B in annual revenue, 200 stores, an e-commerce site, a mobile app and 12M loyalty members.

- **Current state:** no AI in production. Some teams use free public tools informally, and vendors have pitched "AI-powered" personalization, but nothing has been bought.
- **The trigger:** a national competitor launched an AI personal shopper, and the board asked the CEO for the company's AI strategy.
- **The task:** brief the board in two weeks on three questions.

## The three board questions

1. **What is AI?** Software that predicts (predictive AI), creates (generative AI) or acts (agentic AI). It is a capability, not a product to switch on.
2. **What can we realistically do?** Start where value and feasibility are both high. Hold the personal shopper until data and pilots support it.
3. **What should the next 90 days hold?** Three phases, each ending in a gate: scale, adjust or stop.

## What an AI transition involves

1. **Find the real starting point:** current AI use, data quality, vendor claims.
2. **Set guardrails first:** AI policy, approved tools, customer data consent, human review.
3. **Choose use cases by value and feasibility.**
4. **Pilot small:** measure every pilot against a baseline.
5. **Decide on evidence:** only proven pilots earn budget.

It also needs an executive owner, a cross-functional steering group, training and clear data rules.

## Use case portfolio

| # | Use case | Horizon |

| 1 | Product descriptions and content | Now (0-90 days) |
| 2 | Service agent-assist | Now |
| 3 | Store associate knowledge assistant | Now |
| 4 | Outfit and product recommendations | Now (pilot) |
| 5 | Loyalty offer personalization | Next (3-9 months) |
| 6 | Demand forecasting and allocation | Next |
| 7 | Markdown and pricing optimization | Later (9-18 months) |
| 8 | AI personal shopper | Later |

## The 90-day plan

| Phase | Focus | Gate |
|-------|-------|------|
| Days 1-30 | Align and baseline: owner, interim AI policy, inventory, four pilots chosen | Policy live, pilots chosen, measures agreed |
| Days 31-60 | Build and test: internal pilots, one consented recommendation pilot, vendor tests | Pilots live, no open privacy or brand issues |
| Days 61-90 | Prove and decide: controlled tests, costed scale-up, 12-month roadmap | Board decides to scale, adjust or stop |

## Key risks

Customer data and consent, staff using public AI tools, wrong or off-brand generated content, vendor hype, low adoption, and chasing the competitor too early. Each has a mitigation in the briefing.
